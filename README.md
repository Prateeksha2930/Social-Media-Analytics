Social Media Analytics Dashboards & Performance Reports

Project Overview
This repository contains a suite of social media analytics dashboards and data visualization projects developed during my tenure at Onni Creative. The primary objective was to transform raw cross-platform data into actionable insights to optimize digital presence and client engagement strategies.

By leveraging advanced Excel techniques and PowerPoint visualization, I built comprehensive monitoring systems that track KPIs across Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn.

Key Projects
1. Multi-Platform Master Client Dashboard
Developed a high-level "Master Client" data visualization report designed for executive-level stakeholders.

Purpose: Aggregate performance metrics from various digital touchpoints into a single, cohesive narrative.

Key Metrics: Cross-platform reach, consolidated engagement rates, and comparative growth trends.

2. Facebook Profile Performance Dashboard
Created an automated Excel-based dashboard to monitor Facebook Page health.

Features: Automated data entry sheets feeding into a dynamic visual dashboard.

Insights: Deep dives into follower growth, post-type effectiveness, and daily reach fluctuations.

3. Instagram Insights & Analytics
Designed detailed performance trackers for Instagram to understand audience behavior and content resonance.

Functionality: Segregated reporting for "Profile Performance" and "Content Summary".

Visualizations: Time-series analysis of likes, comments, shares, and impressions to identify peak engagement windows.

4. LinkedIn Professional Engagement Reports
Analyzed professional network growth and B2B content performance.

Focus: Tracking organic reach and professional audience demographics.

Technical Skills Applied
Data Aggregation: Consolidating raw exported data from platform-native analytics (Facebook Insights, Instagram Insights) into workable datasets.

Advanced Excel: Utilization of PivotTables, complex formulas, and conditional formatting to create dynamic dashboard interfaces.

Data Visualization: Crafting clean, intuitive charts and slide decks that highlight key takeaways rather than just raw numbers.

Performance Benchmarking: Establishing baseline metrics to calculate month-over-month (MoM) growth and engagement percentages.

Impact at Onni Creative
These dashboards streamlined the weekly and monthly reporting process, reducing the time required to gather insights while increasing the accuracy of data-driven decisions. The visualizations directly influenced content scheduling and ad spend allocation by identifying which creative assets performed best on specific platforms.
